Vice President Kamala Harris has officially named Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz as her vice presidential running mate. Mr. Walz is in his second term as Governor of Minnesota where some accomplishments include:
- Providing universal free school meals for students
- Protecting reproductive freedom
- Strengthening voting rights
- Laying the groundwork to get Minnesota to 100% clean electricity by 2040
- Cutting taxes for the middle class
- Expanding paid leave for Minnesota workers

Vice President Harris and Governor Walz stand for us all in Salem City. Harris-Walz 2024! We will not go back!
