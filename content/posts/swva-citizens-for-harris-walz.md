The Salem Virginia Democratic Committee is excited to announce a community gathering on Saturday, November 2nd, an occasion to come together and celebrate the values that unite our city and reflect the vision of Vice President Kamala Harris. With the arrival of Donald Trump on the same day, we are reminded of the stark contrast between his divisive, extreme politics and the inclusive, hopeful spirit we believe best represents Salem.

We invite our neighbors to join us as we rally for unity, respect, and progress, values embodied by Kamala Harris and by so many here in Salem. Together, we can look forward to a brighter, more compassionate future under her leadership.

Come meet fellow Salem residents on November 2nd as we share our optimism and commitment to moving forward. This is a new day, and we're ready for the journey ahead.
